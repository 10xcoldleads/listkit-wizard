# ListKit category hygiene

ListKit (or Zapier) labels are often wrong. Fix them whenever you open a thread.

## Rules
After reading the full thread:
- Warm future / keep-in-mind / substantive engagement / check-back-later must **not** stay Not Interested → use **Interested**.
- True nah / opt-out → **Unsubscribed**.
- OOO → **Out Of Office**.
- Bounce → **Bounced**.
- Wrong person can stay Not Interested after ack unless they engage.
- Live surplus, bid, auction, or "yes we have X" → **Interested**.

Always leave a clear one-line reason in your own notes when you retag. Recategorize in the same pass as the reply when possible.
