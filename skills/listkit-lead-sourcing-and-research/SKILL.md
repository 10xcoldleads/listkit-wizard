# ListKit lead sourcing and research

Build or expand lists for ListKit campaigns beyond ListKit's own database when needed.

## Scope
Use browser and APIs the operator already has. Keep lead files campaign-ready: **first_name and last_name required** on every row — never email-only uploads. Enrich or skip if names are missing.

## Pipeline
1. Define ICP
2. Build universe
3. Find people + emails; validate ([ListKit email validation](sand-workflow:listkit-email-validation))
4. Personalized research when the sequence needs per-lead copy
5. QC → import ([ListKit campaign launch](sand-workflow:listkit-campaign-launch))

Never invent research facts. Results land in ListKit for send/reply.
